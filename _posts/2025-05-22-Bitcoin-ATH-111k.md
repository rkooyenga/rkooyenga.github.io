Emotion and FOMO (fear of missing out) are likely kicking in. What to do? Jump in blind, no research, no feel for the market, no adjusted plan. Just a sweaty-haired slob with a gin-flushed face rolling those dice. It is an option yes. Seriously, don't do it, lol.

Me on the sidelines? What? Yeah, I know. My roadmap was transparent shared with friends, less so on social media compared to the old days when we'd post all our moves and debate them. Broad strokes are on Twitter/X, though. I talked about targets. I didn't want to speculate $120K; rather, I wanted to see where things went, but I think $110K to $115K was a tentative exit. Can't foresee north of $125K or $130K that far back; for price discovery, you have to be watching.

> BTW, I do like Michael. I left Crypto Twitter a long time ago. But my list of over 1000 is now a couple of people, so he's a real one.

 A lesson is you won't hit the exact bottom or top. You can be the first out that fire escape, even if everyone looks at you funny. That's actually a good sign in my experience. You aren't running in fear or concerned with the crowd; you're following a step in your plan that outlined doing 'x' in consideration of 'y' and competing with the crowd. That's what matters. There are no points for risking death, seeing how close you can get your neck to the sword. If you're a foot away and hit your targets, just walk away.

Buy back in for me was at the highest, mid 90 range. I'm mostly very liquid. I setup some alerts and automation to help, new APIs, looking for signals on a couple of key accounts' socials, sentiment checks, historical comparisons, and very exciting, action-packed data for the heart of it (the election) through March. That was fun, actually. Too much at once given all the app projects. Then news happens, direction changes, and I spend two days writing custom scripts pausing everythings else without hesitation as this is instant funding for eveything else right? And planning on 1 to 15 days, but in mere hours you get your answer, exciting. 

In the end my head's a mess. Something really shook me this month; such that it changes everything or nothing, I don't know. I just stopped caring about almost anything. Like a fuse blew. So, yes, I abandoned my own plan at end and everything else I was excited for. None of what I built for analysis and alerting, is even running. Hell, I don't remember the apps I was releasing the day the fuse blew; that's what I was doing, a promo video. for a coupld things and I went blank 3 weeks later still dont know or care. Lucky I remember my name. 

Rough guess probably out $15K, minimum $10K, the cost of not paying attention anymore. I don't "feel" the regret I should. No FOMO. Not really remorse. I feel blank. Like it's very bad. I know. But there's a perspective here timeline has cahnged on lots of things look at my age. The end goal is gone. Honestly, winning just means more time on Earth with people I don't like. Who enabled or even directly caused this. 

Another shame is I wanted to plot it—a theoretically sexy chart. But without a finish, it's far less interesting. Still, I'm proud of two years of smart moves, of rigid adherence to basic rules It's stuff we probably already know, but sometimes you need to get your ass kicked to really apply. And that basic stuff means more than any chart TA wizardy. You're trading against yourself and your emotions. Your weaknesses are the greatest opponent and I beat that opponent except this last round which at least proves the rule. Because I lost a lot because of ....me! Telling you, simplest trading rules will help a lot.
