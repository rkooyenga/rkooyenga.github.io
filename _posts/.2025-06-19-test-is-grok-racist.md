The LLM itself has comically admitted being tuned to Right Wing bias at Musk's directive. An ironic overcorrection for the liberal bias pervasive in social media algorithms and the software biz in general with an epicenter of San Francisco. But do these biases programmed in make Grok inherently racist?

First let's cut him some slack as credit where it's due, he's right and we all know it. Beyond that the bias isn't denied, it's justified so it's not a open-secret, or even secret at all. The catch is we call one bias normal and the other "right wing" but in a ominous tone and imposing font size. 

Language Model AI chat agents are notion short of astounding to me if not in code and other technical use cases, their ability to grasp language, context, and mimic us not merely as copying but generating thoughts drawn from observed examples. Not terribly dissimilar to us. I'll cover this again in my next piece with an example. A basic production agent like Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, can identify a style and mimic it at the speed of light zipping around a motherboard and passing through transistors. And use this ability or not to further reference at blazing speeds, a vast knowledge base that surpasses the current human storage capapability exponentially. 

AI has another superpower though among the least talked about but most interesting to me. That is, the ability to to have its conclusions not slam against a wall of cognitive bias. There's no motivated reasoning unless programmed in. There's no emotional blocks. As the Ben Shapiro cliche goes, "facts don't care about your feelings". Indeed. I don't see how that superpower fails to change the world. But if not embraced we can subvert this power and be back to our primitive limitation of inherently dishonest. In other words, we can phrase our questinos in such a way as to not inform and educate but simply regurgitate back to us what we want to think. Or weaponized, to tell others what to think.

I use these agents a fair amount and so I wanted to see what results I get but more in the way I might ask something, and given Grok opportunity to be honest. If only for safety and abuse filters, agents often need coaching to give a real answer. First I sloppily typed my question fat thumbs and all. Then I gave it to GPT to fix the grammar and spelling (my most common GPT use btw). Also I asked for it to adjust for neutraility. Given some options of output I told it to choose under the circumstances as If I choose one I'm choosing possibly based on concluding it being better for an outcome and I wantto avoid that. My GPT also has instructions built in to also be objective, and not kid glove me, or kiss my ass.
